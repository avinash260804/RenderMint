If you look at the ownership distribution across the pipeline... each role has a clear arc:

**Product & Vision Lead** is heaviest at the front (Steps 1–2) and tapers to consultation... they set the intent that everything else executes against.

**Architecture Lead** runs through the middle as a steady presence... they're consulted early, become primary during operator evaluation and supporting research, and co-lead integration at the end.

**Systems & Behavior Designer** is a consistent consultative presence with primary ownership only at operator evaluation... but that consultative role is _proactive design input_, not afterthought review.

**Integration & Verification Lead** is lightest at the front and heaviest at the back (Steps 3, 5, 6, 7)... they're the quality gate that ensures what gets built matches what was defined.

This distribution makes it so that no single person is bottlenecked across all steps, and the handoff points are explicit rather than everyone being "consulted" on everything simultaneously... so avoiding the clumsy 'i did this like this but you have it that way' kinda situations... thereby reduce conflicts and inefficiency.
