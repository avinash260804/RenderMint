## UI Audit Checklist

- Is the hierarchy clear?
- Is the spacing intentional?
- Is the density appropriate for the zone?
- Does the page feel like a creative-professional platform, not a generic SaaS dashboard?
- Are empty states useful?
- Is mobile behavior clear?
- Are interactions accessible?
- Are animations meaningful rather than decorative?
