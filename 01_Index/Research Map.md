## How to process research

Use this flow:

1. Capture the source in a research note.
2. Summarize what it is.
3. Explain why it matters for Atelier.
4. Decide what to adopt.
5. Decide what to avoid.
6. Link it to product, UI, feature, security, or Codex maps.
7. Convert useful conclusions into a decision note or Codex handoff.
