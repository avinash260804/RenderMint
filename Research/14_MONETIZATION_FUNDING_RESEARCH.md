# Atelier Monetization and Funding Research

# Research Document Context

These files are created for **Atelier / Designers Hub**, a creative-professional community platform being reworked around forum discussions, structured critique, help posts, resources, profiles, dashboards, uploads, search, reputation, settings, moderation, and future admin systems.

The purpose of these files is not to provide final answers yet. Each file clearly defines **what data must be researched**, **why it matters**, **what questions to answer**, and **what output should be produced** before implementation decisions are made.

Use these files as research briefs for ChatGPT, Codex, manual web research, competitor analysis, product planning, and implementation planning.


## Purpose

This research explores how Atelier can eventually fund servers, tools, moderation, storage, and development without damaging community trust.

## Main research objective

Research ethical funding models for a creative community platform and decide what should be considered now, later, or avoided.

## Data to research

### 1. Cost structure

Research expected costs:

- Hosting.
- Supabase database/auth/storage if used.
- Cloudflare/R2 storage and bandwidth.
- Upstash Redis.
- Email sending.
- Analytics.
- Domain.
- Monitoring/logging.
- Future AI features.
- Moderation/admin effort.

Data to collect:

- Current free-tier limits.
- What happens after free tier.
- Cost triggers.
- Monthly cost estimates at small, medium, and larger usage.

### 2. Funding model options

Research possible models:

- Donations/supporter model.
- Freemium membership.
- Premium profiles.
- Pro critique tools.
- Portfolio enhancements.
- Job board.
- Sponsored challenges.
- Workshops/courses.
- Design resource marketplace later.
- Ethical sponsorships.
- Grants/community funding.

For each model, research:

- Revenue potential.
- Community risk.
- Implementation complexity.
- Trust impact.
- Whether it fits Atelier.
- Whether it should launch now or later.

### 3. What should remain free

Research what must stay free for community health:

- Basic posting.
- Basic commenting.
- Asking for help.
- Giving critique.
- Reading public posts.
- Basic profile.
- Joining discipline hubs.

Data to collect:

- What paywalls would hurt growth.
- What features are safe to monetize later.

### 4. Premium feature ideas

Research possible premium features:

- Advanced profile customization.
- Portfolio case-study blocks.
- Private critique rooms.
- Advanced saved collections.
- Better analytics for public profile.
- Custom domain profile later.
- Professional portfolio review events.
- Extended upload limits.

Data to collect:

- Which features users might pay for.
- Which features should not be paywalled.
- Which features are too early.

### 5. Job/opportunity model

Research:

- Design job boards.
- Freelance opportunity boards.
- Creative gigs.
- Paid job posting.
- Featured opportunities.
- Community trust and spam risks.

Data to collect:

- Whether job board fits Atelier.
- How to prevent low-quality job posts.
- Whether job board should be future roadmap.

### 6. Sponsorships and partnerships

Research:

- Tool sponsorships.
- Design challenge sponsors.
- Education partners.
- Workshop partners.
- Newsletter sponsorships.

Data to collect:

- What sponsorships are acceptable.
- What should be avoided.
- How to disclose sponsorships.

### 7. Monetization risks

Research what can damage the community:

- Aggressive ads.
- Selling user data.
- Paywalling participation.
- Boosted posts that distort quality.
- Paid reputation.
- Sponsor influence over moderation.
- Overbuilding monetization before community exists.

## Questions this research must answer

1. What are Atelier's likely costs?
2. What funding model fits the platform best?
3. What should remain free forever?
4. What could become premium later?
5. Should jobs/opportunities be part of the model?
6. Are sponsorships acceptable?
7. What monetization would damage trust?
8. What should not be built now?
9. What should be prepared structurally for later?
10. What is the safest first funding path?
