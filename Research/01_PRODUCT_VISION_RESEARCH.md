# Atelier Product Vision Research

# Research Document Context

These files are created for **Atelier / Designers Hub**, a creative-professional community platform being reworked around forum discussions, structured critique, help posts, resources, profiles, dashboards, uploads, search, reputation, settings, moderation, and future admin systems.

The purpose of these files is not to provide final answers yet. Each file clearly defines **what data must be researched**, **why it matters**, **what questions to answer**, and **what output should be produced** before implementation decisions are made.

Use these files as research briefs for ChatGPT, Codex, manual web research, competitor analysis, product planning, and implementation planning.


## Purpose

This research defines what Atelier should become at the product level. It should prevent the platform from becoming a generic forum, generic SaaS dashboard, or ordinary portfolio site.

## Main research objective

Research how Atelier can become a premium creative-professional community where beginners, students, designers, and experienced creators can discuss, critique, learn, showcase, and build reputation through meaningful contribution.

## Data to research

### 1. Creative professional needs

Research what different creative users actually need:

- Beginner designers looking for direction.
- Students needing critique and project feedback.
- Self-taught creators needing community validation.
- Experienced designers wanting deeper discussion.
- Professionals wanting reputation and visibility.
- Creators looking for resource sharing and peer learning.
- Users who feel existing communities give generic answers.

Collect data on:

- What problems they face when asking for feedback.
- Why they avoid posting work publicly.
- What makes feedback trustworthy.
- What kind of recognition feels meaningful.
- What makes them return to a community.
- What makes them leave a community.

### 2. Product positioning

Research how Atelier should be positioned compared to:

- Forum platforms.
- Portfolio platforms.
- Q&A platforms.
- Design inspiration platforms.
- Discord/Slack communities.
- Social media design spaces.
- Learning/course communities.

Collect data on:

- What each platform type does well.
- What each platform type fails to provide.
- What gap Atelier can fill.
- What should be unique about Atelier.
- How Atelier can avoid copying existing platforms.

### 3. Product promise

Research what promise Atelier should make to users.

Possible promises to evaluate:

- Get better design feedback.
- Build creative reputation.
- Find discipline-specific answers.
- Join a serious design community.
- Learn through structured critique.
- Share work and improve through iteration.
- Move beyond generic Q&A.

For each promise, research:

- Is it clear?
- Is it believable?
- Is it valuable?
- Can the product actually deliver it?
- Does it attract the right community?

### 4. Product principles

Research principles that should guide every future decision.

Topics to research:

- Quality over quantity.
- Structured critique over random comments.
- Discipline-specific context over generic answers.
- Creator identity over anonymous posting.
- Reputation through contribution, not clout.
- Learning through iteration.
- Community standards over chaotic engagement.
- Premium but accessible UI.
- Beginner-friendly but professional.

### 5. Core product pillars

Research and define the main pillars of Atelier:

- Discussion.
- Critique.
- Help/Q&A.
- Resources.
- Showcase.
- Profiles.
- Reputation.
- Discipline hubs.
- Moderation.
- Admin/settings.

For each pillar, research:

- Why it exists.
- Who uses it.
- What user action it supports.
- What UI it needs.
- What data it needs.
- What could go wrong.
- What should be avoided.

## Questions this research must answer

1. What is Atelier in one sentence?
2. What is Atelier not?
3. Who is the first ideal user?
4. What is the main reason someone would join?
5. What is the main reason someone would return?
6. What is the main reason someone would contribute?
7. Why should an experienced creator spend time here?
8. Why should a beginner trust this space?
9. How is Atelier different from Reddit, Discord, Dribbble, Behance, Stack Overflow, and normal forums?
10. What product principles should never be compromised?
