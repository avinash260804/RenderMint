# Atelier Moderation Research

# Research Document Context

## Widely used moderation strategies today

Most serious online communities converge on a similar toolkit and process. We can use these as building blocks, then adapt them to Atelier’s creative, beginner-first context.

## Clear rules and Code of Conduct

Best-practice guides emphasize a **short, clear, easily accessible Code of Conduct** that defines expected and unacceptable behaviors, who the community is for, and how members should handle conflicts.higherlogic+1  
Rules are typically reviewed by legal counsel and presented prominently (site footer, homepage, onboarding), with forced acceptance at signup to reduce “I didn’t know” violations.higherlogic+1

## Onboarding as moderation

Many communities reduce violations by using onboarding to teach norms before people post.[watchers](https://watchers.io/post/online-community-moderation)  
Examples include requiring members to answer a few questions about why they’re joining and to explicitly confirm guidelines via a checkbox, plus welcome emails and pinned posts that recap key rules.[watchers](https://watchers.io/post/online-community-moderation)

## Reporting and user controls

Effective moderation relies on **community reporting** because moderators can’t see everything at scale.helpshift+1  
Standard patterns include a prominent “Report” button, category-based report reasons, and user-level controls such as block/mute/ignore so members can manage their own experience.[watchers](https://watchers.io/post/online-community-moderation)

## Escalation paths and “strike” systems

Guides recommend a **consistent escalation ladder**, often a “three-strike” policy: educate on first violation, warn and place under closer review on second, and suspend or ban on third, with clear communication each time.higherlogic+1  
Consistency (same violation → same consequence) is repeatedly cited as essential for trust; bending rules for specific users is warned against.higherlogic+1

## Automation: keyword filters and pre‑moderation

Modern platforms use automation to reduce moderator load and catch obvious problems faster.utopiaanalytics+1  
Common tools include keyword filters and watch-word lists that flag or auto-hide content before publication, pre‑moderation queues for sensitive spaces, spam detection, and auto-ban triggers for clear-cut violations like NSFW or link spam.utopiaanalytics+1

## Moderator roles and training

Research emphasizes that moderation is a **learnable skill** requiring training, scripts, and emotional support.helpshift+2  
Best practices include having a core staff team plus trained volunteer moderators, shared playbooks, and internal norms (e.g., be calm and polite, explain rule decisions, avoid deleting borderline content without explanation).higherlogic+1

## Community self‑moderation

Mature communities aim to become partly self-moderated: members model good behavior, call out problems, and use peer-to-peer moderation tools.helpshift+1  
Platforms like Reddit and Stack Exchange rely heavily on trusted members with elevated tools and voting systems to surface quality content and suppress low-effort contributions.helpshift+1

## Common pain points in mainstream moderation

To design Atelier’s journey, we need to critique where mainstream moderation often fails—especially for beginners and learning-focused spaces.

## 3. Inconsistent enforcement and perceived bias

Several sources emphasize the importance of **consistent rule enforcement**, because changing what’s allowed or bending rules for certain members causes frustration and distrust.higherlogic+1  
In a creative setting, inconsistency can also show up as “taste-based” removals—content removed because a moderator personally dislikes it, which Reddit’s own mod guidelines explicitly warn against.[reddit](https://www.reddit.com/wiki/moddiquette)

## 4. Opaque decisions and poor communication

Users often experience moderation as mysterious: content disappears, bans occur, but explanations are lacking.higherlogic+1  
This creates confusion and prevents learning; members don’t know how to adjust their behavior, which is especially harmful in an educational platform where feedback should improve future posts.[Atelier-Principles.md](https://ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/web/direct-files/collection_0db86406-4ebc-4e05-9ee0-667033dc739f/fd24bf27-cd40-4491-a54a-0fdaad915cd9/Atelier-Principles.md?AWSAccessKeyId=ASIA2F3EMEYERFETSCFZ&Signature=wz1Z8fJDy%2B6s1etBBpgfIi4AsCA%3D&x-amz-security-token=IQoJb3JpZ2luX2VjEG4aCXVzLWVhc3QtMSJGMEQCID1o3YKW2kxGUVeelyCFj0cd7wv3GXcf0YfFE4ay8ZhBAiB7faC66%2F9hPdgOh1u0N%2B8a97yBSV2NL%2FgW0D2GpoQvOirzBAg3EAEaDDY5OTc1MzMwOTcwNSIM6ojmaNUKjBIqMywnKtAE2fxknr%2F1Vh9N3EIYTX6JYYbwoQR9O%2Bh47k3qoyqbe0wJ3bmojjuDZyEMjpvEXf8e3TGQzaGYsQKk3pC6RLMImVVGLCC7MEe54rnuSBomg4pfgCxTaQP3h14kxdnklD%2FdBGsOZNgmJVHgQb%2F%2B5sHJS3jq5tFg3I3sgl0cRiUwFqkXQkKf%2Fhy6%2BtLzilrpppLSe5WPTWt7MMRgQsJYAv4OIK4cFvgtRd8ZxjJkK2o8xKyApFUz5GFLOo9McLNm%2BNrun3kg0Raa89T2FOfzau3%2FlBHms6L431WhZ0UkQfpIKDSiRp2sKNrFYPChKMNT9FsJe8OkpKmW5s1XifKpGD5E8QvnB9l3ffPyLetZZCqCzDqDnWEpdQUGKojSUZcuzU5JvCHmL1OKQx0jCwRnKKto%2F9CE5kKTeBYhVktgkWLgrT%2BV5xC03XDs6KsN37p%2BYVEoTGMz%2BcFkGWYt6C6R2G6XphHMZ3PCxyD01GMSjayJG5erJPLYH84IXyFI5EXIeycbtdAqRFT8CO7aCE8lHK12oMJR8mlqfwRzdkl6sGrLzh%2FscGHK1uOHwi5WCTVC5nhQwdupwOPZdv%2FcB30QvyZQ81moxI5wobyjKX%2FoHLdyF3fssTdlwsHMkJjtOhMDV2G9sGKEFy3bc%2FcDIjYzV5E%2F30PhDdwuLLjcT%2FzYSLyX6I6Apd0Xih32%2FMTXH8NVY9lJgwcWDvK3iz3Y0AWDdZIRlYW6T%2FUVwFv6NeCrvYSGcZajd2tQQjdPaeoJwwkBm99x6RIogiYGbZFb8Vih4O6XjDC9mpjTBjqZAVOZV%2F9htQkLIjRdqwoTUJ036blA9c%2Bdz%2FqUb%2BHhkIqPosbkEsuGvJIjs1OMdwxp54t5z%2BlYLXi9Vk3lofytyV7jjHswix6FRO3g9UwS%2FUwCENHx20cH35uJShftCU%2FtBdgLIlgG11QzuenzK%2BgzBaczdr4Eizus90pcAth7C8JGUGpVbUsKOs8CoRZSc5ATfkW%2BNLh1BH%2FFRw%3D%3D&Expires=1785076496)

## 6. Moderator burnout and lack of support

Moderation involves constant exposure to conflict and potentially harmful content, and many guides note that moderators need emotional support, clear procedures, and realistic workloads.helpshift+1  
Burned-out moderators may respond harshly, become inconsistent, or disengage, all of which hurt community health.

Many systems focus on catching violations but invest less in rewarding helpful behavior; best-practice guides now recommend actively rewarding good contributions.higherlogic+1

## Design principles for Atelier’s moderation

Adapting from the above and from the Atelier charter, we can define moderation as a **learning-focused quality system** rather than just a policing function.

## 3. Escalation ladder aligned with learning

Borrowing from “three-strike” systems but adapting language:

1. **Gentle quality nudge** (Strike 1 – Educate):
    
    - Private message or inline notice explaining which guideline was missed, with links to examples of good posts.
        
    - Post may be set to “Needs Revision” rather than deleted, giving the user a chance to fix it.helpshift+1
        
2. **Monitored participation** (Strike 2 – Warn):
    
    - User’s new posts enter a pre-moderation queue or are visible but flagged for closer review; they receive a clearer warning.[higherlogic](https://www.higherlogic.com/blog/community-moderation-best-practices/)
        
    - An optional short call or message from a moderator to explain why their pattern is problematic, especially for recurring shallow critiques or dismissive tone.[helpshift](https://www.helpshift.com/blog/6-tips-for-a-successful-online-community-moderation/)
        
3. **Timeout or suspension** (Strike 3 – Restrict):
    
    - Temporary timeouts for repeated minor issues (e.g., 7–30 days), and permanent bans for serious violations (hate speech, harassment, doxxing, explicit NSFW).higherlogic+1
        
    - Clear closing message explaining the decision and, when appropriate, conditions for return.
        

Throughout, communication is **educational** and **respectful**, reflecting Atelier’s professional tone while protecting community safety.[helpshift](https://www.helpshift.com/blog/6-tips-for-a-successful-online-community-moderation/)[Atelier-Principles.md](https://ppl-ai-file-upload.s3.amazonaws.com/web/direct-files/collection_0db86406-4ebc-4e05-9ee0-667033dc739f/fd24bf27-cd40-4491-a54a-0fdaad915cd9/Atelier-Principles.md?AWSAccessKeyId=ASIA2F3EMEYERFETSCFZ&Signature=wz1Z8fJDy%2B6s1etBBpgfIi4AsCA%3D&x-amz-security-token=IQoJb3JpZ2luX2VjEG4aCXVzLWVhc3QtMSJGMEQCID1o3YKW2kxGUVeelyCFj0cd7wv3GXcf0YfFE4ay8ZhBAiB7faC66%2F9hPdgOh1u0N%2B8a97yBSV2NL%2FgW0D2GpoQvOirzBAg3EAEaDDY5OTc1MzMwOTcwNSIM6ojmaNUKjBIqMywnKtAE2fxknr%2F1Vh9N3EIYTX6JYYbwoQR9O%2Bh47k3qoyqbe0wJ3bmojjuDZyEMjpvEXf8e3TGQzaGYsQKk3pC6RLMImVVGLCC7MEe54rnuSBomg4pfgCxTaQP3h14kxdnklD%2FdBGsOZNgmJVHgQb%2F%2B5sHJS3jq5tFg3I3sgl0cRiUwFqkXQkKf%2Fhy6%2BtLzilrpppLSe5WPTWt7MMRgQsJYAv4OIK4cFvgtRd8ZxjJkK2o8xKyApFUz5GFLOo9McLNm%2BNrun3kg0Raa89T2FOfzau3%2FlBHms6L431WhZ0UkQfpIKDSiRp2sKNrFYPChKMNT9FsJe8OkpKmW5s1XifKpGD5E8QvnB9l3ffPyLetZZCqCzDqDnWEpdQUGKojSUZcuzU5JvCHmL1OKQx0jCwRnKKto%2F9CE5kKTeBYhVktgkWLgrT%2BV5xC03XDs6KsN37p%2BYVEoTGMz%2BcFkGWYt6C6R2G6XphHMZ3PCxyD01GMSjayJG5erJPLYH84IXyFI5EXIeycbtdAqRFT8CO7aCE8lHK12oMJR8mlqfwRzdkl6sGrLzh%2FscGHK1uOHwi5WCTVC5nhQwdupwOPZdv%2FcB30QvyZQ81moxI5wobyjKX%2FoHLdyF3fssTdlwsHMkJjtOhMDV2G9sGKEFy3bc%2FcDIjYzV5E%2F30PhDdwuLLjcT%2FzYSLyX6I6Apd0Xih32%2FMTXH8NVY9lJgwcWDvK3iz3Y0AWDdZIRlYW6T%2FUVwFv6NeCrvYSGcZajd2tQQjdPaeoJwwkBm99x6RIogiYGbZFb8Vih4O6XjDC9mpjTBjqZAVOZV%2F9htQkLIjRdqwoTUJ036blA9c%2Bdz%2FqUb%2BHhkIqPosbkEsuGvJIjs1OMdwxp54t5z%2BlYLXi9Vk3lofytyV7jjHswix6FRO3g9UwS%2FUwCENHx20cH35uJShftCU%2FtBdgLIlgG11QzuenzK%2BgzBaczdr4Eizus90pcAth7C8JGUGpVbUsKOs8CoRZSc5ATfkW%2BNLh1BH%2FFRw%3D%3D&Expires=1785076496)
