Here's the complete Showcase framework, structured the same way as your Odyssey definition doc, and built on top of Atelier's existing Spotlight, Honor, and Profile systems.[^1][^2][^3]

## Purpose

Showcase is the final-publishing portfolio and recognition space for completed creative work — it doubles as an inspirational gallery for the whole platform. It's deliberately distinct from Critique, which is for work-in-progress feedback; Showcase only holds "graduated" work, and every piece feeds directly back into the creator's profile so it can speak for them without explanation.[^2]

## User Types

Four groups use this section differently: beginners publishing for the first time, regular practitioners, mentors/senior creatives with Featured or judging privileges, and casual viewers/browsers (logged-in or not). Beginners get a simple nudge to publish after their first critique cycle, while Mentor+ Honor levels unlock Verified badges, Featured rotation eligibility, and endorsement powers.[^3][^2]

## Entry States

Users land on Showcase through the global nav tab, the Explore/landing page featured hero, their own profile ("Your Spotlight"), shared deep links, or a direct CTA after finishing a critique cycle. Entry is always Studio-filtered by default but one click away from platform-wide browsing, and logged-out visitors see it on the landing page purely as social proof.[^2]

## First-Time Journey

The ideal first session is: browse the grid, open one project, react, click through to the creator's profile, and optionally publish a first piece. No case-study write-up is required at first publish — just an image and a one-line intent statement — keeping the "time to first value" short and avoiding feature tours.[^2]

## Core Components

- Showcase grid: full-bleed cinematic thumbnails, no stat clutter
- Featured hero rotation: weekly staff pick or algorithmic high-Honor pick[^2]
- Project detail page with an iteration/before-after toggle
- Creator signature strip pulled live from the profile, no separate bio needed
- Reaction bar, Studio/discipline filters, and a shareable Honor Card link[^2]


## Content Types

The core unit is a Final Project (image/video plus intent statement), optionally linked to its originating Critique thread as Iteration History, alongside Collections (curated project groups) and Featured/Staff Picks. Linking a project to its critique history creates a visible before-to-final transformation record — this is Atelier's key differentiator from Behance or Dribbble.[^2]

## Actions and Permissions

| Action | Who can do it | Gate |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| View, react, save, follow | Everyone, any Honor level | None — core actions never blocked [^2] |
| Publish a project | Any user who completed a Critique cycle | Quality gate, not an Honor gate |
| Feature/pin/curate | Studio Moderators, Global Mods | Role-based [^2] |
| Endorse, judge Challenges | Sapphire+ (Honor 1000+) | Honor gate [^2] |

## Progressive Disclosure Layers

Showcase reveals depth in three layers, mirroring the Direct → Adjacent → Unexpected discovery model: Layer 1 is pure visual browsing (image, title, creator), Layer 2 adds project depth (intent, tags, software), and Layer 3 surfaces process depth (iteration history, endorsements, Honor Card, related cross-Studio work). New and casual viewers naturally stay at Layer 1; engaged users drift deeper through clicking, not onboarding prompts.[^1]

## Moderation Logic

Publishing requires a quality gate (the project must trace back to a completed or critiqued piece of work), backed by spam/plagiarism filters and a report flow for stolen or low-effort content. High-visibility slots like Featured or the landing hero require minimum positive-signal thresholds, and unclear submissions get a "needs more context" nudge rather than outright removal, consistent with Atelier's guide-first moderation philosophy.[^3]

## Success Metrics

Track publish rate (share of users completing a critique cycle who then publish), reaction quality distribution (Inspired/Insightful/Precise vs raw views), profile click-throughs from Showcase, Featured rotation diversity, and Honor growth tied to Showcase activity. Raw view or like counts should be avoided as headline KPIs since they contradict the "Knowledge over Virality" principle.[^3][^2]
